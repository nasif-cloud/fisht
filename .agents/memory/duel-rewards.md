---
name: Duel rewards
description: Eligibility rules for the daily duel victory reward
---

The qualified duel winner can receive 30 XP and 1,000 Beli once per daily reset window. A reward requires the opponent to be within winner level -5 through winner level +30, and the accepted match must be no later than the winner's third duel against that same opponent in the current Eastern Time month. Duel participation and daily quest progress remain unlimited and separate from reward eligibility.

**Why:** Players should be able to duel freely without farming the daily payout through repeated matches or extreme level gaps.

**How to apply:** Record accepted matches per opponent before combat starts, keep the daily reward timestamp separate from duel activity, and treat failed count persistence as ineligible rather than awarding without a recorded match. Respect the winner's duel-reward DM setting when notifying them.

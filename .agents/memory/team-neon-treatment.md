---
name: Team neon treatment
description: Material rules for the team image borders and background
---

Team visuals should use the custom game-interface treatment: solid `#12131C` slate with a subtle 20px grid, a flat 3px `#FFD166` featured border, flat 2px `#4A5568` side borders, 6px corners, attached `#1A202C` name bars, and flat rank badges.

**Why:** The user explicitly chose a developer-crafted game UI over the earlier neon treatment and rejected glow-heavy, particle-based, pill-overlay styling.

**How to apply:** Preserve the solid colors, flat borders, 6px geometry, all-caps Trebuchet-style typography, gold 2px outlined power number, attached name bars, and top-left tier badges. Avoid `shadowBlur`, drop shadows, starfields, and decorative spill.

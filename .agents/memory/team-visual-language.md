---
name: Team visual language
description: Visual direction for rendered team images
---

Team images use the supplied transparent fire-ring asset as the card frame, with circular cropped card art inside it, copy-count captions in the form `Co. N Card Name`, and a dark warm charcoal background.

**Why:** This keeps the team presentation tied to the user's provided art direction instead of generic rectangular card UI.

**How to apply:** Preserve the circular fire-frame composition and caption convention when adjusting team image layout, typography, or card states.
